# Phase 07.1 Plan 04: QALD-9-plus Vendoring + D-06 Filter + DBpedia Ontology Subset Summary

**Vendored QALD-9-plus's combined English DBpedia train+test pool (558 raw questions), ran a two-pass D-06 filter (parse -> provisional-transpile -> ontology authoring -> final harness-default transpile) that kept 514 (24 parse-fail, 20 genuine transpiler-feature-gap drops), and authored a 75-class/250-property DBpedia ontology subset programmatically from the survivors' own referenced terms -- landing QALD-9-plus's achieved MDE at ~0.055-0.062, within D-02's ~6-8pt target.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~90 min (including empirical data analysis to determine the real term surface before authoring the ontology)
- **Tasks:** 2 completed
- **Files modified:** 11 (10 created, 1 modified)

## Accomplishments

- Fetched `data/qald_9_plus_{train,test}_dbpedia.json` from `KGQA/QALD_9_plus` @ `b9fb0380902e6935efc9505170bc3652e8c242c1`, pruned to an English-only extract (id + first `en` string + gold SPARQL, dropping all other-language paraphrases and `answers` blobs), and authored a CC-BY-4.0 `NOTICE.md` with full attribution, source URL, commit, and a "changes made" note.
- Empirically measured (rather than assumed) the real filter-survival numbers: of 558 combined train+test questions, 534 parse via rdflib (24 fail — malformed `COUNT`/missing-prefix gold, a known QALD-9 noise pattern per RESEARCH Pitfall 4) and 514 of those transpile under a bootstrap (empty-ontology, permissive-class) resolver (20 hit a genuine `UnsupportedSparqlError`/`AqlEmitError` — mostly "UNION whose arms bind no new variables").
- Extracted the 514 survivors' full schema-term surface by walking every BGP triple in their translated algebra: 75 distinct classes (64 `dbo:`, 10 `dbyago:`, 1 `owl:Thing`) and 250 distinct properties, classified object-vs-datatype by triple-object-type evidence (Literal/URIRef/typed-Variable).
- Authored `dbpedia_subset.ttl` programmatically from those measured terms: 75 `owl:Class` (+ `phys:collectionName`), 88 `owl:ObjectProperty` (+ `phys:edgeCollectionName`), 162 `owl:DatatypeProperty` — disambiguating 19 local-name collisions (e.g. `dbo:starring`/`dbp:starring`) in the `phys:` string values only.
- Ran the final D-06 pass at harness-default settings (`permissive_class_resolution=False`) against the authored subset: all 514 provisional survivors transpiled to non-empty AQL (0 `term-not-in-subset` drops — the ontology was built from exactly their own terms).
- `corpus.yml` carries the `ontology:` block + 514 cases (`qald9plus-{train,test}-{id}` qualified names); `filter_log.md` reconciles 514 kept + 44 dropped == 558 total, with a per-reason breakdown and a D-07 achieved-MDE note.
- `test_convert_qald.py` (12 tests, TDD RED->GREEN): unit tests on `_first_english`'s deterministic FIRST-en selection, an integration pass over the real vendored pool asserting every kept case validates as a `CorpusCase`, filter-log reconciliation, run-to-run determinism, ontology resolvability via `SchemaResolver.from_turtle`, a no-`yaml.load(` static guard, and a drift guard comparing the committed `corpus.yml`/`dbpedia_subset.ttl` against what `convert()` produces right now from the checked-in raw files.
