# Phase 07.1: NL→SPARQL synthetic eval-corpus growth - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-07-22
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Grow the **held-out NL→SPARQL eval corpus** (and its **disjoint** few-shot bank)
from 25 cases to **real statistical power** — target **minimum-detectable-effect
(MDE) ≤ 5pt** (down from ~16pt today) — via **ontology-driven synthetic
generation** of curated (natural-language question → gold-SPARQL) pairs, so that
future NL-quality lifts become measurable at all. Phase 07's dense/BM25 sweep
showed BM25 few-shot delivers a real lift but that the 25-case corpus is too small
to confirm most effects (the pre-registered dense-vs-zero test was an underpowered
null). This phase removes that measurement ceiling.

**In scope:** eval-corpus growth + disjoint few-shot-bank expansion, driven by a
committed, seeded, ontology-driven generator over a **CDF-shaped, self-contained**
conceptual schema; machine-generated golds (parse + transpile by construction) with
LLM-authored natural language, human-curated via English-only faithfulness review;
regenerate the scripted `baseline.json` + headroom invariant; prepare the live
re-baseline runbook.

**Explicitly OUT of scope (deferred to a v1.1 NL milestone):** generator
fine-tuning (SFT / LoRA / QLoRA), execution-guided / validator-in-the-loop
selection, retriever-embedder fine-tuning, and any modern-embedder / hybrid-fusion
retrieval swap. Those become *measurable* only once this corpus growth lands, which
is why they wait.

**Non-regression (hard invariants):** the deterministic SPARQL→AQL transpiler is
untouched; W3C DAWG query-eval coverage stays **≥ 96.4%**; `scripted` stays the
no-network CI default; the committed bank↔corpus disjointness gate (D-02 from Phase
07) stays green.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Growth target & statistical power (Area: Growth target)
- **D-01: Fix MDE, derive N.** The target is locked as **MDE ≤ 5pt**; the required
  held-out case count **N is derived from a power analysis**, not guessed. Use the
  same test family Phase 07 pre-registered — **paired McNemar** — computed at the
  observed baseline pass-rate, driven by the **discordant-pair proportion** (McNemar
  power depends on discordant pairs, not raw N). The researcher/planner runs the
  power calc and states the assumptions; the corpus grows to the resulting N.
- **D-02: Grow incrementally until MDE ≤ 5pt is empirically met (no blind static N).**
  Human curation is the cost driver, but removing the measurement ceiling is the whole
  point, so accept the cost rather than truncating the target. **Refined after research
  (07.1-RESEARCH.md, Pattern 1 / Open Q1):** the power analysis on THIS repo's own
  Phase-07 discordant-pair data (π ≈ 0.24–0.28) puts MDE ≤ 5pt at **N ≈ 630–785**
  (π=0.20 optimistic → 628; π=0.25 observed → 785), materially above the original
  "low hundreds" guess. User decision (2026-07-22): keep the ≤ 5pt target but **grow in
  batches (~+150–200), re-measure the *achieved* MDE on each gated live sweep (inverse
  of the Connor formula at the observed N/π), and STOP as soon as ≤ 5pt is met** —
  which may land below 785 if the real discordant rate is favorable. Ceiling sized to
  ~630–785. This operationalizes "grow until met" literally and de-risks the π
  assumption (RESEARCH Open Q2 → incremental adopted).
- **D-02a: Few-shot bank sizing (RESEARCH Open Q3 → adopted).** The bank has no
  statistical-power role (retrieval diversity only); size it at **~25% of the total
  generated pool**, covering the same difficulty-class taxonomy with per-class density
  for retrieval — not derived from a power calc.
- **D-03: Rebalance the difficulty mix toward failing classes for headroom.**
  Over-weight the classes where models actually fail (hard property paths, multi-hop,
  negative/`expect_refusal`) rather than scaling today's mix proportionally.
  Statistical power comes from **discordant pairs**, which require cases that models
  get wrong — so headroom (baseline pass-rate meaningfully < 1.0) must be preserved,
  enforced by the existing headroom-invariant check.

### Synthesis mechanism (Area: Synthesis mechanism)
- **D-04: Hybrid generation — templated gold + LLM natural language.** Enumerate
  gold-SPARQL shapes **deterministically over the ontology** (controllable difficulty,
  guaranteed to parse and transpile), then have an **LLM write the natural-language
  question** for each templated gold. Human-curated. Combines valid golds with
  natural-sounding questions.
- **D-05: Machine-generated golds only — the LLM never authors the gold.** The gold
  (the judged target) comes from the templated enumeration, so every gold parses
  (rdflib) and transpiles by construction. This eliminates hallucinated/subtly-wrong
  targets — the correctness anchor is deterministic.
- **D-06: Committed, seeded generator.** The ontology-driven generator is **checked
  in and deterministic** (pinned seed) so the corpus is regenerable and auditable.
  The LLM natural-language step runs **gated/offline** (like the live sweep — not in
  CI), and its outputs are committed as trusted repo data. Mirrors the repo's
  provenance discipline (`corpus_sha`, pinned model id + revision).

### Ontology surface (Area: Ontology surface)
- **D-07: Extend the eval fixture ontology into a CDF-shaped, self-contained
  conceptual schema.** The ~7-term `:Person`/`:Order` fixture is too small to
  enumerate ~N genuinely-distinct cases (near-duplicates add no power and trip the
  cosine-similarity ceiling). Grow it into a slightly larger, **human-readable
  OWL/RDFS conceptual schema** (more entities + datatype attributes + object-property
  edges + typed collections) that **resembles the shape CDF / Onto-Extract emit** —
  the *favorable* case per the research (human-readable schemas vastly outscore
  opaque Wikidata-style IDs). This is the research's own **1-hop ontology-extension**
  technique (which grew a corpus 15 → ~513 pairs).
  - **Scope guard — self-contained, NOT coupled to live CDF.** The ontology stays a
    self-contained Turtle block **inside the eval fixtures** (`corpus.yml`, byte-
    identical in `fewshot_bank.yml`), parsed only by the eval harness via
    `SchemaResolver.from_turtle`. It is **not** wired to the live, evolving CDF master
    ontology and **not** grounded in a specific customer domain (e.g. Zscaler
    Customer360) — coupling this fixed regression corpus to an external evolving
    artifact would risk churn and untranspilable constructs. "CDF-shaped" means
    *representative in form*, not *sourced from CDF*.
- **D-08: Stay within proven `phys` idioms only.** New terms use exclusively the
  physical-mapping idioms already exercised: datatype property → attribute, object
  property → edge collection (`phys:edgeCollectionName`), typed collection
  (`phys:collectionName` / `phys:typeField` / `phys:typeValue`). Every gold
  transpiles with **zero transpiler work**. The corpus measures **NL→SPARQL quality**,
  not transpiler breadth (that is Phase 1's job and its gate is untouched).

### Curation & gold validity (Area: Curation + disjointness)
- **D-09: English-only faithfulness curation (curator is not SPARQL-fluent).** The
  curator cannot verify NL↔gold faithfulness by reading SPARQL, so the workflow must
  not require it. Because golds are **template-generated**, the generator also emits
  a **canonical plain-English gloss** of each gold (e.g. "Count the orders placed by
  each person"). Faithfulness review is then **English-to-English**: does the LLM's
  natural question mean the same as the gloss? An **automated LLM-judge pre-screens**
  the NL-vs-gloss match; the human confirms/rejects in plain English. Gold *validity*
  is guaranteed separately by the deterministic parse + transpile gate
  (`test_gold_transpilable.py`), so no SPARQL literacy is needed at any step.

### Disjointness at scale (Area: Curation + disjointness)
- **D-10: Bank↔corpus disjointness holds by construction.** As both pools grow to
  the hundreds, partition the generation space so the eval corpus and the few-shot
  bank draw from **structurally disjoint slices** (different entity subsets / template
  families). Disjointness holds by design rather than by post-hoc filtering (which
  would thrash against the cosine-similarity ceiling at scale). The committed 3-way
  gate (`test_fewshot_bank_disjoint.py`: normalized question text + canonical algebra
  of the gold + cosine-similarity ceiling) stays the **backstop**, not the primary
  mechanism. This preserves Phase 07's D-01/D-02 held-out guarantee.

### Baseline scope (Area: Curation + disjointness)
- **D-11: Regenerate scripted in-phase; live re-baseline is human-run, documented.**
  This phase **regenerates the scripted `baseline.json` + headroom invariant** against
  the grown corpus in-phase (deterministic, CI). It **pins N from the D-01 power
  analysis under stated assumptions** and **prepares the live re-baseline** (new/
  updated `configs.yml` entries + runbook). The credentialed human runs the live
  sweep **out-of-band** and folds it in — mirroring Phase 07's documented-completion
  discipline. Live sweeps never run in CI. (Rejected: requiring a fresh in-phase live
  baseline, which would couple phase completion to credential/human availability.)

### Claude's Discretion
- **Exact N and per-class counts** — output of the D-01 power analysis + D-03
  rebalancing; planner/research sizes them and justifies against the headroom
  invariant.
- **Generator design details** — template grammar, seed management, ontology-term
  enumeration order, and how the plain-English gloss is emitted from each template.
- **LLM used for the NL-authoring step and the faithfulness pre-screen** — planner's
  call, consistent with the repo's provider conventions; must be a gated/offline
  authoring-time step, not a CI dependency.
- **New ontology term choice** — which concrete CDF-shaped entities/attributes/edges
  to add, within D-07/D-08 constraints.

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- **CDF context (background provenance, not a repo dependency):** "CDF" = the
  **Contextual Data Fabric** Arango initiative (ontology-as-product; a federated-query
  layer that maps English → ontology concepts → AQL/SQL). `arango-sparql-py`'s
  NL→SPARQL is a building block that layer feeds, so the eval ontology should *resemble*
  the human-readable conceptual schemas CDF / Onto-Extract emit — hence D-07's
  "CDF-shaped" framing. Source notes live in the user's vault
  (`~/Desktop/the-vault/contextual-data-fabric-*`, module specs `M1`–`M9`); they are
  **background only** — downstream agents do not read them and this corpus is not
  coupled to them.
- **The power insight that shapes the whole phase:** McNemar power is driven by the
  **discordant-pair proportion**, not raw N — which is *why* D-03 rebalances toward
  failing classes. More easy cases the model already passes add N but not power.
- **The curation unlock:** template-generated golds → a canonical plain-English gloss →
  English-to-English faithfulness review is what lets a non-SPARQL-fluent curator stay
  the human in the loop (D-09).

</specifics>
