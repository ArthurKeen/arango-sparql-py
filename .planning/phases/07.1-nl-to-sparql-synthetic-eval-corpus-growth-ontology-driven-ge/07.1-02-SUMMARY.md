## Decisions Made
- Commit/version token regex accepts either a 7-40 char hex SHA or a `vX.Y[.Z]`-shaped version string, since different vendored sets may pin by commit hash (QALD-9-plus, CK25 are both GitHub repos with commit SHAs) or by release version — no reason to force one convention.
- Kept the secret-pattern list to the plan-specified minimum (`sk-`, `AKIA`, PEM private-key header, `Bearer `) rather than expanding scope to a general secret-scanning library — matches the plan's explicit acceptance criteria and the RESEARCH.md "Known Threat Patterns" static-grep recommendation.
- Read files with `errors="strict"` inside a `try/except (UnicodeDecodeError, OSError)` rather than `errors="ignore"`, so a binary file's garbled-but-decodable bytes can't slip past the scan without at least attempting a strict decode first — a stricter defensive posture than the plan's `read_first` note technically required, applied because it costs nothing and closes a small gap.
