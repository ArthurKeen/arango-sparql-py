## Decisions Made
- Used the empirically-observed exception messages (via a scratch probe against `arango_sparql.api.translate()` and then `NlPipeline`) rather than the RESEARCH.md table's guessed message text, since two of the research candidates' exact wording turned out to differ slightly from what actually fires (see `key-decisions` in frontmatter for the SERVICE and OPTIONAL-non-variable-object specifics)

Used the OPTIONAL-with-UNION-body trigger instead to reach the required 6-new-case minimum.
- Skipped re-authoring an "inverse-arm negated path" case (a research candidate) since it would duplicate the already-existing `neg-inverse-arm-negated-path`, not add new coverage.
